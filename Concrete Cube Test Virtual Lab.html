DATABANK: MS EN 12390

1. SPECIMEN SIZE

STANDARD CUBES ARE 150MM X 150MM X 150MM. CROSS-SECTIONAL AREA IS 22,500 MM².

2. COMPACTION

FILL IN 3 EQUAL LAYERS. TAMP UNIFORMLY WITH A STANDARD ROD (AT LEAST 35 STROKES PER LAYER FOR A 150MM CUBE).

3. CURING

DEMOULD AFTER 24±2 HOURS. SUBMERGE IN CURING TANK AT 20±2°C UNTIL TEST AGE (7 OR 28 DAYS).

4. TESTING

APPLY LOAD CONTINUOUSLY WITHOUT SHOCK. COMPRESSIVE STRENGTH = MAX LOAD (N) / AREA (MM²).
